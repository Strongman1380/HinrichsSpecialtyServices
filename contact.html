Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with organizations outside of Nebraska?

Absolutely! While we're based in Nebraska, our digital services are available nationwide, and we can provide remote consultation for business development.

What is included in the $150 website plan?

Up to five hours each month for progressive development, hosting, maintenance, security updates, routine content updates, basic SEO, and Search Console monitoring.

How are new websites completed?

New websites are built progressively through the five included hours each month. Timing depends on scope and current capacity, and HSST confirms the plan before promising a completion date.

What if I need more than five hours?

Unfinished work can continue with the next month's allocation. Extra work is available only with advance approval at $30-$65 per hour based on complexity.
