We accept Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, check, wire, cash, or another agreed method. Contact HSST for the correct instructions.

Work pauses or continues with the next month's allocation. Faster work can be approved in advance at $30-$65 per hour, depending on complexity.

Timing depends on the current workload and the work requested. HSST confirms the starting plan before committing to a delivery date.

Absolutely. You receive all files, code, credentials, and content. No vendor lock-in, ever.
