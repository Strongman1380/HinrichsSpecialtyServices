Privacy Policy

How we collect, utilize, protect, and store your information.

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

At Hinrichs Specialty Services and Technology (HSST), we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our clients, community members, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you interact with our website (hsstech.com / hsst.com) and our consulting or digital services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum amount of information necessary to deliver our services, process requests, and improve your user experience. The types of information we collect include:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact and Inquiry Forms: When you contact us for a quote, consultation, or message us through our forms, we collect your name, email address, phone number, company/organization name, budget details, timeline, and the contents of your message.
  • Membership Enrollment & Services: If you enroll in our programs or subscribe to our community resources, we collect your name, contact information, role, interests, goals, and referral sources.
  • Domestic Violence Accountability Program (Class Payments): For participants in our specialized accountability programs, we collect intake details, student name, email address, phone number, and class progress data.

B. Payment Processing Information

When you make weekly class payments, pay intake fees, or purchase digital solutions, transactions are processed securely through Stripe (our third-party payment processor). HSST does not store or have direct access to your credit card number, bank details, or CVV. Stripe handles this information in accordance with their secure PCI-DSS standards.

C. Automatically Collected Information

To analyze visitor traffic without compromising your privacy, we utilize Plausible Analytics. Plausible is a privacy-first web analytics tool. It does not use cookies, does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII), and does not track you across other websites. All data is aggregated and anonymized.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following business and operational purposes:

  • To provide, manage, and deliver the consulting and digital solutions you request.
  • To respond to your inquiries, schedule consultations, and provide customer support.
  • To process transactions and send payment receipts.
  • To send you our newsletter, digital tip sheets, and updates, provided you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To maintain the safety, security, and integrity of our digital platforms.
  • To meet legal obligations, court requirements (such as attendance and payment logs for accountability programs), or regulatory standards.

3. How Information is Stored and Secured

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your data:

  • Database Security: Stored information is held in secure databases (such as Cloud Firestore and PostgreSQL) protected by strict security rules, access controls, and encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Data Retention: We retain personal information only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.
  • No Selling of Data: We will never sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services We Use

We partner with selected third-party service providers to help run our business. These companies are authorized to use your personal information only as necessary to provide these services to us:

  • Stripe: Payment processing and billing.
  • Web3Forms: Handling and delivering contact form submissions securely.
  • Plausible Analytics: Aggregated, privacy-friendly website usage analytics.
  • Firebase/Vercel: Website hosting and cloud database infrastructure.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You have control over your personal data. Depending on your location and context, you have the following rights:

  • Opt-Out of Communications: You can opt-out of receiving newsletter or marketing emails from us by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any email or by contacting us directly.
  • Access and Deletion: You may request to see what personal information we hold about you, or request that we update or delete it. Please contact us to make this request.
  • Legal Program Records: Note that for court-mandated domestic violence accountability classes, certain records (such as class attendance, enrollment, and payment history) must be maintained for legal compliance and reporting to probation/courts and cannot be deleted upon request.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
