## Design Context

### Users
Small-business owners, nonprofit leaders, and prospective service clients who need a dependable website, practical technology support, or virtual assistance without managing the work themselves. They need to understand the offer quickly, trust the business, ask questions, and make contact.

### Brand Personality
Practical, capable, and human. The experience should build confidence through clarity and responsiveness, with enough technical polish to feel current without looking flashy or impersonal.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing dark navy, blue, and orange brand identity and recognizable HSST artwork. Refine it into a trust-first technology studio aesthetic with a strong visual hierarchy, asymmetrical editorial compositions, and restrained motion. The public site remains dark-themed. The CRM remains a clear, functional administrative workspace.

### Design Principles
- Explain real services and pricing plainly. Do not rely on invented metrics or exaggerated claims.
- Use motion to establish hierarchy, tell the service story, and confirm interaction. Never make motion decorative noise.
- Preserve accessibility, strong contrast, responsive behavior, reduced-motion support, and clear focus states.
- Keep the HSST logo and brand colors recognizable while reducing excessive glow, particles, glass effects, and repeated card grids.
- Make primary actions obvious: understand the plan, ask a question, contact HSST, or sign into the CRM.
